Four surfaces. One accessibility audit tool. All live this week.

I have spent the last year building AMASAMYA. It is the audit tool I always wanted as a blind accessibility architect: everything works from the keyboard, screen readers announce every state change with meaning, and the results table tells me which component broke, not just that something did. Nothing about it is retrofitted for accessibility. Accessibility is the product.

What is on it:

WCAG 2.2 conformance audits, per-page and site-wide.

GIGW 3.0 and IS 17802 India-national engines. I could not find another auditor that runs these. The Indian government accessibility standards deserve tooling that matches the WCAG side of the world.

VPAT 2.4 ACR one-click export, so compliance conversations start with a document, not a spreadsheet.

Visual audit diffs, so you can compare two runs and see what regressed.

On Android, the app audits native Android apps too through the AccessibilityService API. Live TalkBack captions, focus-trail visualiser, touch-target boundary mapper, contrast drift scanner, one-click fix suggestions, TalkBack simulator mode for sighted testers.

Try it on a real page you audit every week. Not a demo page. Something in production. Tell me what breaks. That is where the next version starts.

If your team runs WCAG audits, GIGW audits, VPAT reporting, or accessibility QA on a release cadence, the extensions are free and take under a minute to install. The Android app is also free and does the same thing for native Android UIs.
